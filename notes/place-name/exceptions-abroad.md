# Traditional Hebrew names of towns abroad: attestations

Compiled 2026-11-04. Read-only research; no project files changed except this note.

## Method and labels

Most evidence comes from the Sefaria search API (field `exact`). It returns hit counts and fragments from **modern digital transcriptions of printed editions**, so VERIFIED means "seen in that transcription", not in a scan or a manuscript. A later printing may have normalized the spelling. Hit counts are rough: they include modern works and, for short words, unrelated words (e.g. פריש "separated", ליון "to Greece", שפירא as a surname, בקושטא "in truth"). URL of any reference: `https://www.sefaria.org/<Ref>` with spaces as underscores.

Two other sources were used:
- **Davidson**: I. Davidson, *Oṣar ha-shira we-ha-piyyuṭ* (1924–33), its source list as transcribed on Hebrew Wikisource (`אוצר השירה והפיוט/מפתח המקורות/מקורות עברים`). These are Davidson's bibliographic descriptions of title pages. They are close to the title pages but may be normalized. Labelled VERIFIED (Davidson).
- The notes already in this folder (`evidence-french.md`, `evidence-german-yiddish.md`, `evidence-spanish-english.md`), which were compiled the same way. Forms taken from them without re-checking are marked "(prior note)".

Labels: **VERIFIED** (seen in a text transcription, as stated), **REPORTED** (secondary source only), **UNKNOWN**. Periods are those of the author or the document, not of the printing.

No scans were inspected in this round. hebrewbooks.org title pages and Commons ketubbot were not reached in the time available. Hebrew Wikisource answered a few queries and then returned HTTP 429 (rate limit).
