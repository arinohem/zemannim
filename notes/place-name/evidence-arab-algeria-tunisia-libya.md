# Traditional Hebrew spellings: Algeria, Tunisia, Libya (research notes, 2026-11-05)

Read-only research; no project files changed.

## Sources and method

- **Sefaria** full-text search (helper `sefaria_search.py`), plus full downloads of *Sefer ha-Tashbeṣ* I–IV and *Teshuvot ha-Rivash* via the Sefaria API. I extracted the short heading lines (the addressee town written above each responsum) and tallied them. Sefaria hit counts are raw totals and include unrelated words (e.g. בונה = "builds"), so the snippets were read before counting anything.
- **Princeton Geniza Project** (geniza.princeton.edu): search of the transcriptions, mostly 11th–12th c. Judaeo-Arabic. Each count is the number of documents. Some hits are in the modern Hebrew *translations* stored with the transcriptions; I have marked these where I saw them.
- **English Wikipedia langlinks to Hebrew Wikipedia**: modern Israeli forms only, used as a fallback.
- I could not use: Hebrew Wikipedia full text (HTTP 429), the NLI catalogue and ketubba collection, hebrewbooks full text, or Attal's bibliography. I found no lists of town names for gittin. Web searches in Hebrew returned little.
- Labels: VERIFIED = read in a Sefaria transcription (not a scan) or a Geniza PGP transcription; REPORTED = taken from a secondary or catalogue source; INFERRED; UNKNOWN.
- Dating: Rivash (d. 1408) and Tashbeṣ (Shimʿon b. Ṣemaḥ Duran, d. 1444) wrote in Algiers. Tashbeṣ IV (Ḥuṭ ha-meshullash) belongs to the Duran family, 15th c. (REPORTED, general knowledge; I did not check it on a title page).

## Re-checks of forms already chosen

- Tunis **תונס**: confirmed and the most common pre-modern form. תוניס is mostly 20th c.
- Tripoli **טריפולי**: has a pre-20th-c. attestation in Livorno printing ("טריפולי המערב"). Local usage was טראבלס.
- Algiers **אלגזאיר**: confirmed as the Duran form (15th c.). אלגיר and ארג'יל are later; אלג'יר is modern.
- Constantine **קסנטינה**: attested (2 headings), but **קסנטינא** is more frequent in the Tashbeṣ (6 + 3 for קוסנטינא).
- Oran **והראן**: confirmed (15 headings).

## Gaps

I found no lists of town names for gittin, no ketubbot, and no Attal or Hirschberg data. Djerba, the Tunisian Sahel towns, and the Libyan towns other than Tripoli still need checks in NLI imprints and in the Tunisian and Djerban responsa, which are not on Sefaria.
